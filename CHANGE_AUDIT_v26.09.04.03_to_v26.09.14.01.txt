HV P2P v26.09.04.03 -> v26.09.14.01 CHANGE AUDIT
========================================================================

Purpose
-------
v26.09.14.01 adds SRVR-authoritative automatic network firmware convergence for
CTRL and W1P while preserving the approved UI, motion semantics and established
safety/recovery paths from v26.09.04.03.

Primary intentional changes
---------------------------
1. SRVR read-only firmware authority module and exact immutable bundle validator.
2. Shared CTRL/W1P Ethernet OTA client with manifest, role, hardware target,
   release, exact size, complete SHA-256 and embedded identity validation.
3. CTRL startup authority/E-stop hold and sequencing gate preventing CTRL-TS OTA
   until CTRL itself exactly matches SRVR.
4. W1P startup authority hold integrated with existing Servo Enable/drive safety,
   with OTA flash writes only after the unchanged stopped/braked service gate.
5. SRVR W1P parser treats missing/false FW_MATCH fail-closed.
6. Native firmware builder creates/verifies one exact SRVR_FIRMWARE_BUNDLE after
   CTRL-TS -> staged CTRL -> final CTRL -> W1P compilation.
7. All Mac Intel, Mac Apple Silicon and Windows x64 SRVR jobs depend on that
   firmware job and package the exact same verified bundle.
8. Firmware authority HTTP, immutable bundle-builder and automatic-OTA contracts
   are integrated into the master source regression suite.
9. One-time bootstrap/recovery documentation added.
