-----------------------
- Virtual uses real CTRL/CTRL-TS input but simulates position/speed inside SRVR.
- No non-zero physical W1P VEL can be emitted through SRVR _send_velocity while Virtual is active.
- A present W1P is positively held with STOP + SW_SRVON 0, periodically refreshed.
- W1P/EL7 health is not a Virtual demo E-stop source; CTRL/SRVR input/safety remains authoritative.
- Physical W1P POS_M/VEL_MPS cannot overwrite the Virtual state; Virtual re-reference does not send physical SYNC_POS.
- Returning to Encoder sets the existing joystick-neutral/Servo Enable re-arm interlock before physical motion can resume.
